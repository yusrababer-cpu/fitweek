Your 5-day weekly workout plan

Five different ways to train, so your whole body gets what it needs. Two rest days.

Monday

Upper body strength, 35 min

  • Incline push-ups, 3 x 10
  • Dumbbell or bottle rows, 3 x 10
  • Shoulder press, 3 x 10
  • Biceps curls, 2 x 12
Meal idea: 2-egg veggie omelette with 1 slice of whole-wheat toast, about 300 kcal
Tuesday

Cardio and core, 30 min

  • Brisk walk or march, 5 min
  • Intervals: 1 min fast, 2 min easy, x 5
  • Plank, 3 x 20 seconds
  • Dead bug, 3 x 8 each side
Meal idea: red lentil soup (1 bowl, about 240 kcal) with 1 roti and salad, about 380 kcal
Wednesday

Yoga, mobility and meditation, 40 min

  • Gentle sun salutations, 10 min
  • Hip and hamstring stretches, 10 min
  • Spine and shoulder mobility, 10 min
  • Breathing meditation, 10 min
Snack idea: plain yogurt, berries and walnuts, about 180 kcal
Thursday

Lower body strength, 35 min

  • Squats to a chair, 3 x 12
  • Glute bridges, 3 x 12
  • Step-ups, 3 x 8 each leg
  • Calf raises, 2 x 15
Dinner idea: grilled chicken, rice and salad, about 450 kcal
Friday

Full body circuit and Tai Chi, 35 min

  • 3 rounds: squats, push-ups, rows, marching, 30 seconds each
  • Tai Chi cool-down, 10 min
  • Body-scan meditation, 5 min
Breakfast idea: oats with banana, milk and chia seeds, about 350 kcal
Saturday and Sunday

Rest days

Rest is part of the plan. Add an easy walk if you like.

Why mix strength, yoga, Tai Chi and meditation?

Strength training

Simple bodyweight and light-weight moves that build muscle and everyday energy. Every move has an easier option.

Yoga

Gentle stretching and breathing to ease tension and improve flexibility.

Tai Chi

Slow, flowing movement that improves balance and brings calm.

Meditation

Short breathing and body-scan practices to ease stress and help you rest.
